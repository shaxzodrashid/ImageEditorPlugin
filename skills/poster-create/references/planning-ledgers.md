# Planning ledgers

Complete these ledgers before composing. Keep them with the task notes or project artifacts.

## Poster plan

| Poster | Purpose | Takeaway | Approved copy | CTA | Brand anchors | Safe-zone anchors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Make each row communicate one primary idea. Skip any poster that has no independent purpose.

## Asset ledger

| Asset ID | Poster and placement | Subject | Source | Owner | Rights basis | Identity check | Transform | Unique |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Record canonical source-page URLs, not search thumbnails. “Rights basis” must distinguish supplied/approved, official press use, licensed stock, public domain, or unresolved.

Treat these as the same source asset: alternate resolutions, crops, cutouts, mirrors, rotations, recolors, blurs, masks, relights, background extractions, and generated variations derived from one output. Assign every creative source to only one placement across the complete deliverable.

Brand identity files and standardized arrow glyphs are system primitives and may repeat only when the brand/task requires them.

## Layout fingerprints

| Poster | Dominant subject | Copy block | Alignment axis | Reading path | Major shapes | Negative space |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Compare every poster pair. Fail a pair when its spatial skeleton is substantially the same, even if the copy, color, crop, or source image differs. Maintain campaign coherence through typography, color, spacing logic, and tone rather than repeated composition.
