# Planning ledgers

Complete these ledgers before composing. Keep them with the task notes or project artifacts.

## Design-system extraction

| Evidence | Rule or behavior | Source/page | Mandatory, permitted, example, or locked | Implementation effect | Conflict/missing asset |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Include rendered visual examples, not only text citations. Resolve canvas, background, template, logo, type, and safe-zone authority before ideation.

## Recent-work fingerprints

| Prior output | Concept premise | Hero treatment | Scale/crop | Dominant axis | Copy relationship | Visual device | Palette emphasis | Avoid or retain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Inspect the latest three to five relevant outputs when available. Preserve intentional brand invariants; avoid repeating the same creative combination by habit.

## Concept slate

| Direction | Concept sentence | Message link | Hero/evidence | Spatial premise | Brand expression | Medium | Feasibility | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Create four to six directions and thumbnail the strongest three. Record why the selected direction won and why each rejected direction lost.

## Campaign rhythm

| Invariants across the set | Planned variables | Repeating hero/motif and reason | Hook | Explanation | Proof/contrast | Payoff | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Keep two to four invariants. Vary only what strengthens the story; do not equate unrelated imagery with creativity.

## Poster plan

| Poster | Purpose | Takeaway | Approved copy | CTA | Visual thesis | Visual-copy relationship | Brand anchors | Safe-zone anchors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Make each row communicate one primary idea. Skip any poster that has no independent purpose.

## Asset ledger

| Asset ID | Poster and placement | Subject | Source | Owner | Rights basis | Identity check | Transform | Reuse class/rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Record canonical source-page URLs, not search thumbnails. “Rights basis” must distinguish supplied/approved, official press use, licensed stock, public domain, or unresolved.

Classify reuse as `unique`, `campaign continuity`, `exact-product necessity`, or `system primitive`. Crops, cutouts, mirrors, recolors, masks, relights, and generated derivatives remain the same source lineage. Reuse is valid only when it advances recognition, truth, or story; repeated static placements and lazy derivatives fail.

Brand identity files and standardized navigation glyphs are system primitives and may repeat when required.

## Layout fingerprints

| Poster | Dominant subject | Copy relationship | Alignment axis | Reading path | Major shapes | Negative space | Deliberate invariant | Deliberate variation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Compare neighboring slides first, then the complete set. Fail accidental sameness and random incoherence. Controlled repetition may carry identity; each slide still needs a purposeful visual beat.
